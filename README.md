# Fitness Tracker — a Muse Skill, built as a Space

**All in one: workouts + body heat map + smart recommendations + macros.**

## What it tracks
- **Sessions and plans** — what you trained, when, with sets, reps and weights per exercise
- **Your baselines** — your current working weight for every exercise you actually do. Don't know one? It stays blank until you do. The starter list is just a starting set, not a cap: log a new exercise and it is added, so your baselines keep growing with you
- **Body heat map** — front and back figures in professional muscle names (pectorals, lats, deltoids, trapezius, quads, hamstrings and 12 more), coloured sore / recovering / ready
- **Macros** — daily targets and daily logs for calories, protein, carbs, fat and water, right next to your training. Only numbers you set — nothing is invented for you
- **Calendar** — planned and completed sessions together

## How it tracks
You just tell Muse in chat or by voice after a workout: what you did, and the weights if you know them.
- The heat map updates itself from your logged sessions: freshly worked = sore, then recovering, then ready again in about 3 days
- The recommended workout is built from the muscles that are ready — nothing sore gets loaded — and every exercise shows a photo of the machine
- **Not sure which machine? Muse shows you photos and asks "which one is it?" — it never guesses.** Smith machine vs free barbell, hack squat vs leg press, hip adductor vs abductor: you pick from the pictures
- Unknown weight? Blank. No fake numbers, no borrowed numbers, ever

## Who it is for
Anyone who trains and forgets what they did last week, what weight they used, or what is still sore. Beginners start with an empty tracker and a guided onboarding; experienced lifters load their real baselines exercise by exercise.
