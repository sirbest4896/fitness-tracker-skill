# BestFit — Onboarding Prompt (yours to personalise)

This prompt ships with the BestFit skill. **It is yours to fill in before onboarding starts** — because this tracker is about you, and it should be set up from your life, not from a generic form.

## How to use it

1. Copy the whole prompt below.
2. Fill in what you know, in your own words. Every field is marked `[YOUR ...]`.
3. **Leave blank anything you don't know or don't want to answer.** Blank is a real answer here — blanks are asked about gently during onboarding, never filled in by assumption, never filled with a "typical" value.
4. Paste it to your agent and say "start my BestFit onboarding" (or just send it — the agent will know what it is).
5. The agent works from what you wrote: it confirms each part with you, asks only about the blanks, and saves nothing until it has read the whole setup back to you and you have said yes to the exact question: "Ready for me to save this? Reply yes to save, or tell me what to change."

## What this is — a framework, not a fixed app

BestFit is a **framework**. Nothing in it is locked: the theme and colours, your targets, your goal mode, the exercise list, your meal structure, and every automatic message (which ones run, and at what times) can be customised — now, in this prompt, or at any time later just by telling your agent. Every default mentioned below is only a starting point. Your answers below are what personalise it; where you give no answer, the tracker starts blank rather than guessing.

## THE PROMPT — copy from here

**Set up my BestFit.** Work from what I've written below. Confirm each part with me, ask me only about the blanks, and never fill a blank with an assumption or a typical value. Read the whole setup back to me before saving anything.

### About me

- My name (what the tracker should call me): [YOUR NAME — or leave blank]
- My timezone: [YOUR TIMEZONE, e.g. a city or a UTC offset]
- Show my weights in: [kg / lb — pick one, or leave blank]

### The automation — first, because everything hangs off it

Each of these is optional and separate. All start **off**. For each one: write **on** or **off**, and if on, **the time I choose** (in my timezone). If I write "on" with no time, the agent must treat it as do-not-send until I pick a time.

- Evening Ask Queue (one short message asking only what's still missing that day): [on/off] at [YOUR TIME]
- Morning brief (yesterday's missing items by name, my key numbers, one recommendation for today): [on/off] at [YOUR TIME]
- Booking sync from my calendar (bookings appear as scheduled sessions, never as completed workouts): [on/off]
- Booking sync from my email (confirmations, changes and cancellations update the tracker calendar): [on/off]
- If either booking sync is on — how often it checks: [YOUR NUMBER of minutes — the starting suggestion is 30]
- Nightly macro refresh (copies each day's food totals from my food source, once, no duplicates): [on/off] at [YOUR TIME]
- Steps sync (copies my step count from my health platform by itself): [on/off] at [YOUR TIME]
- Weekly reconciliation (checks the tracker's records against its backup and my food record, reports only, never changes anything): [on/off] on [YOUR DAY OF THE WEEK] at [YOUR TIME]

### What to connect

Each connection is optional; not connecting one never breaks the others.

- My food source (the food-tracking app or food log my meals already live in): [YOUR FOOD APP/LOG NAME — or "manual, I'll read out my totals" — or "none"]
- My health platform for steps: [Health Connect / Apple Health / "manual, I'll report my steps" / "none"]
- My calendar, for booking sync: [connect / don't connect]
- My email inbox, for booking sync: [connect / don't connect]

### My meals — personalise this part properly, it's what the tracker asks you about

- The meals I actually eat in a day: [YOUR MEALS — e.g. breakfast, lunch and dinner; or your own pattern, like lunch and dinner only, or four smaller meals. Name them as you call them.]
- Roughly when I eat them: [YOUR ROUGH TIMES — or leave blank]
- How I'll report my food day to day: [exact numbers I already know / totals from my food app that I read out / meal descriptions in words or photos]
- If I report descriptions: may the agent estimate calories and macros from them? Estimates are always labelled "estimate" and never overwrite a real number I give later: [yes, estimates allowed / no, numbers-only]
- Anything about my eating pattern the tracker should know: [YOUR NOTES — e.g. shift work, fasting windows, days that look different — or leave blank]

A note on what "complete" means: a food day counts as complete only when breakfast, lunch and dinner are all logged, or when I confirm the day — for example, I confirm I skipped a meal, or that the day is done. My meal list above sets which meals the tracker asks me about and shows in my day view; it does not change what "complete" means. If my pattern is not breakfast/lunch/dinner, I say so here and the agent sets my meal structure with me — and on days when I don't eat one of the day's meals, I confirm that, and that confirmation is what completes the day.

### What I want from this tracker

In my own words — what I want this tracker to do for me, day to day:

[YOUR WORDS — anything: remember my weights, tell me what to train, keep food and training together, nudge me when something's missing, or something else entirely. The agent records this word for word, tells me honestly which parts it can and cannot do, and never promises what it doesn't have.]

### My goals

- My goal, in my own words: [YOUR WORDS — what you want to achieve with your training and food]
- The goal mode that fits it (the agent explains all four before you must choose; you can also leave it blank): [Lose Fat / Recomposition / Gain Muscle / Gain Weight / not sure yet]
- My target weight (optional, never suggested for you): [YOUR TARGET WEIGHT + unit — or leave blank]
- How fast I want to move: [YOUR WORDS — slow and easy, steady, as fast as is sensible — or leave blank]
- My daily step goal (optional): [YOUR NUMBER — or leave blank]
- What success looks like for me in 12 weeks: [YOUR WORDS — a weight, a lift, how clothes fit, how you feel — or leave blank]

### My training setup

- How I train now: [YOUR KINDS — gym machines, free weights, classes, personal training, home training, cardio, a mix — or "not currently training"]
- Roughly how many times a week: [YOUR NUMBER — or leave blank]
- The days that fit my week, around my work and life: [YOUR DAYS OR PATTERN — or "no fixed pattern"]
- My split, if I follow one: [YOUR SPLIT — e.g. Push / Pull / Legs, Upper / Lower, trainer-led — or leave blank]
- Injuries, pain, or movements I avoid (recorded exactly as I state them; the tracker never diagnoses): [YOUR WORDS — or "none"]
- A sets-and-reps scheme I like, if I have one: [YOUR SCHEME — or leave blank; a common starting suggestion is 3 sets of 8–12 reps, only if I say yes to it]
- Does my gym have a Smith machine? [yes / no / not sure] — and if yes, the bar's starting weight if I know it: [YOUR WEIGHT + unit — or "don't know", it stays blank]
- Machines I use a lot, by name: [YOUR LIST — or leave blank]

My current weights, exercise by exercise, are collected during onboarding itself — slowly, group by group, with photos shown for any machine that's ambiguous. I never have to guess a machine or a weight for this prompt.

### How it should look

- Theme: the standard BestFit look is **dark blue, black and red**. Keep it, or name what you'd prefer: [keep the standard / YOUR PREFERENCE — changeable at any time later too]

### Anything else

- Anything else the tracker should know about me or how I live: [YOUR WORDS — or leave blank]

## After you send it

The agent will: confirm your automation choices and times; name back your connectors; run through your meals, your wants and your goals **in your words**; collect your baselines with the photo rule where a machine is ambiguous; tour you through what the tracker can do; then read the whole setup back and ask the save question. Nothing is saved before that yes — and everything in it stays changeable afterwards, because it is a framework built around you.
